### OSINT 🔍 <a name="cyberosint"><a/a>
* 💵📚 [**Practical Social Engineering by Joe Gray**](https://nostarch.com/practical-social-engineering) <br>
  If you're into OSINT then do yourself a favour and check out this book. It changed my life :)

* 🆓👨‍💻 [**Bellingcat’s Online Open Source Investigation Toolkit**](https://bellingcat.gitbook.io/toolkit)<br>
  Great place to start if you're looking for a comprehensive list of OSINT tools for your research.

* 🆓🌐 [**OSINT Framework**](https://osintframework.com/) <br>
  A massive list of tools that would certainly be useful during OSINT research. If you're into OSINT then you've probably heard about it already, but it's still worth mentioning.

* 🆓🌐 [**TinEye**](https://tineye.com/) <br>
  Reverse Image Search tool available online. Search over 85 billion images and find where images appear online.

* 🆓🌐 [**WhatsMyName Web**](https://whatsmyname.app/) <br>
  A free tool that searches hundreds of websites and online platforms to identify where a specific username is in use, helping users map digital footprints. It is widely used by cybersecurity professionals, investigators, and researchers to locate public online profiles and gather publicly available information for investigations.

* 🆓🌐 [**SunCalc**](https://www.suncalc.org/) <br>
  SunCalc is a free web-based tool that visualizes the sun's position, sunrise and sunset times, shadow lengths, and other solar data for any location, date, and time.

* 🆓🌐 [**Google's Advanced Search**](https://www.google.com/advanced_search) <br>
  Google Advanced Search is a powerful search interface that helps users refine searches by filtering results using exact phrases, excluded words, languages, regions, file types, domains, and last update dates.

* 🆓🌐 [**Nitter**](https://nitter.net/) <br>
  Nitter is a free, open-source alternative frontend for X (formerly Twitter) that lets users browse public posts without ads, JavaScript tracking, or the need to log in. It is commonly used by privacy-conscious users, researchers, and OSINT investigators to anonymously view profiles, posts, media, and RSS feeds while reducing tracking by the official platform.

* 🆓🌐 [**Osint Dojo**](https://www.osintdojo.com/resources/) <br>
  OSINT Dojo Resources is a comprehensive online directory of free tools, guides, tutorials, and training materials organized by categories such as people searches, geolocation, social media, domains, images, and cryptocurrencies. It serves as a central reference researchers seeking reliable resources to conduct ethical OSINT investigations and develop their investigative skills.

### Cyber Threat Intelligence 🧠

* 💵📚 [**The Lazarus Heist – From Hollywood to High Finance: Inside North Korea’s Global Cyber War by Geoff White**](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Lazarus-Heist-Based-Hit-podcast/dp/0241554276) <br>
  This book uncovers the story of the North Korean hacking organization behind some of the world's most audacious cyberattacks, from the Sony Pictures breach to billion-dollar bank and cryptocurrency heists. The book reveals how cybercrime has become a crucial tool for North Korea to generate revenue, evade sanctions, and project power on the global stage. It's not a technical book but still highly interesting.

* 🆓🌐 [**APT1 Exposing One of China’s Cyber Espionage Units by Mandiant**](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/mandiant-apt1-report.pdf) <br>
  A thorough report on the APT1 which is a Chinese-based hacker group responsible for cyber-espionage on a global scale. Highly interesting.

* 💵📚🇵🇱 [**Era Cyberszpiegów**](https://wydawnictwo.securitum.pl/era-cyberszpiegow)<br>
  Krótko i na temat odnośnie największych zagrożeń w postaci APT. Książka skupia się na 4 państwach: Chinach, Rosji, Koreii Północnej i Iranie. Dobra pozycja start, nie polecam jeżeli zależy Tobie na bardziej szczegółowych informacjach.

* 🆓🌐 [**Kaspersky cyberthreat live map**](https://cybermap.kaspersky.com/)<br>
  As the name suggest it's a live map of cyberthreats. I absolutely love the visuals of this one. There are many more interesting websites that provide live view of ongoing cyberattacks so feel free to look for them. This is simply one of them that I like at from time to time :)
